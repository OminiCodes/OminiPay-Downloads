What's new in 1.0.6 (build 8)

  • Fingerprint app lock, with account-password verification before enabling protection.
  • Fingerprint verification before disabling protection.
  • Fingerprint unlock after reopening when protection is enabled; account-password unlock when it is disabled.
  • Failed or cancelled verification must keep the app locked and leave the setting unchanged.
  • Remembered email after successful sign-in, with a clear option, plus passkey sign-in on supported devices.

How testing works

  • Submit an application. The team reviews it and follows up by email.
  • Use the official web test or Android tester download link only.
  • Test with a dedicated demo account. Demo balances are simulated, not real money.
  • Report each issue with clear steps and the app version.

Protect your account

Never send your password, one-time code, transaction PIN, recovery details, access token, bank/card information or private verification URL in a report.

Important: OminiPay is still in testing. External payment and payout features are not production-ready. Do not deposit real money or use the tester app for live financial transactions.
